  • Placement (decided): inside Trends — a Nutrition | Weight switch; the Weight view has its own Add weight FAB. The Day row is the everyday way in.
  • Logging is one tap: the Day row opens the Weight sheet straight away (no detour through Trends); "See trend →" goes on to Trends · Weight. With Health "Read weight" on, a scale's reading just appears — nothing to type.
  • Several weigh-ins a day are kept with their times. By default the first of the day counts ("counts · first"); "Count this one" in another's ⋮ menu overrides it ("counts · chosen") — e.g. the first was after breakfast, or the scale misread. Removing the chosen one falls back to the first. The ⋮ menu: count this one (not on the one that counts) · edit (entries made here) · remove. Entries from Health can't be edited — Health owns that number — but can be removed or simply not counted.
  • The days in an expanded week open that day's Weight sheet, so an old day is fixed from Trends too.
  • The Weight sheet logs for the day on screen (yesterday's forgotten weigh-in: go to yesterday, tap the row).
  • Daily value = the day's first weigh-in. 7-day average (chart line, headline, Day row) = mean of the weigh-ins in the trailing 7 days, drawn from 3 values on; gaps don't make it jump.
  • Weeks are calendar weeks (Mon–Sun, or the locale's first day): the mean of that week's weigh-ins and its change vs. the week before; the current week says "so far". Tap a week to see its days.
  • Colour comes from the goal (switch above): losing → down is emerald, gaining → up is emerald, the other way amber. No goal → plain numbers, no milestone lines, and the card offers "Set a goal weight".
  • Settings → "Track weight" (off = none of this shows: no Day row, no Nutrition | Weight switch, no Health weight switches).
  • Milestones are your own list (e.g. 89 · 85 · 80) plus BMI lines once height is set; the chart shows those within range, the card the next one.
  • "~5 weeks" on the next milestone only appears when the last 3 weeks' trend points steadily toward it — otherwise just the distance.
  • Sample data: 12 weeks, ≈ −0.45 kg/week with ±0.7 kg daily noise and a few skipped days.
